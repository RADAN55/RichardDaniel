About

Many voices. One room. Every student leaves with more.

I serve ninth through twelfth graders in 90-minute EL blocks at Laurel High School, across Emerging (ELPA21 Levels 1–2) and Progressing (Levels 3–4) tracks. Every lesson runs on a four-lane differentiation model so a newcomer and a near-exit student can sit in the same room and both leave with something they didn't have before.

Outside the classroom I write, code and publish under EL Publishing: a full 36-week curriculum, interactive practice suites, bilingual standards guides, and a book manuscript, Making Them Count: Teaching English Learners in Mississippi's Overlooked Districts. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish and pursuing National Board certification in ENL, with a long view toward EL district coordination.

01🧑‍🏫 Teachers

I have an English learner in my class and I don't teach English. Where do I start?

Post a language goal next to your content objective, give one sentence frame, and let the student talk before writing. That is Monday Move 1, and the Field Guide walks through the rest in plain language.

The EL Field Guide → Monday Moves (36 weekly cards) →
02🧑‍🏫 Teachers

What do ELPA21 levels 1–5 actually mean for my classroom?

Level 1–2 students need visuals, frames and Spanish as a bridge; Level 3–4 students can handle grade-level text with pre-taught vocabulary; a student exits only when every domain reaches Level 4. The lane finder above turns a level into a plan.

Which lane is your student in? → ELPA21 consortium →
03🧑‍🏫 Teachers

Can I grade an English learner the same way as everyone else?

Grade the content, not the English — and grade against the accommodations on the student's Language Service Plan. Reading aloud, extra time, a word-to-word dictionary and modified assignments are documented supports, not favors.

MS EL Command Center · LSP & accommodations →

05📋 Coordinators

What has to be in a student's cumulative folder to survive an MDE monitoring visit?

Home Language Survey, ELPA21 screener and annual scores, a current Language Service Plan with measurable four-domain goals, parent notification letters, quarterly SET reviews, and four years of monitoring after exit. The Command Center has the checklist and timelines.

MS EL Command Center · compliance → Mississippi Department of Education →
06📋 Coordinators

How does EL progress count in the Mississippi accountability model?

The §15 EL Progress component is worth 5% of the model and is computed from the ELPT overall scale score on a five-year trajectory to proficiency — so one-fifth of the gap closed each year earns full credit.

10🏠 Familias

¿Mi hijo tiene que salir de sus clases regulares para recibir apoyo de inglés? / Does my child have to leave regular classes for English support?

No. Los servicios de inglés se suman a las clases regulares; nunca reemplazan matemáticas, ciencias ni historia. Usted puede ver el plan de servicios (LSP) y pedir una reunión en cualquier momento. — No. English services add to regular classes; they never replace math, science or history. You can see the service plan and request a meeting anytime.

11🏠 Familias

¿Cómo puedo ayudar en casa si no hablo inglés? / How can I help at home if I don't speak English?

Lea con su hijo en español, hable de la escuela en español y pídale que le explique lo que aprendió. Un español fuerte acelera el inglés. — Read with your child in Spanish, talk about school in Spanish, and ask them to explain what they learned. Strong Spanish speeds up English.
